# Adjudication and Dice

_How the sources treat the moment a character attempts something and the DM decides what happens: whether to roll, which roll, what difficulty, how rulings relate to rule text, and how failure and status conditions feed forward. Neutral and descriptive: this note lists what the gathered sources cover. It states no verdicts._

## Tags in this area

- `adjudication` — how the DM resolves what happens when a character attempts something
- `when-to-roll` — deciding whether an action needs a roll at all
- `dc-setting` — choosing difficulty numbers
- `ability-checks` — ability checks, skill and tool use
- `saves` — saving throws
- `contests` — opposed rolls
- `rulings-vs-rules` — DM rulings, rule text, rules discussions and mistakes
- `fail-forward` — failure that changes the situation instead of stalling it; consequences of failure and loss
- `conditions-effects` — status conditions and imposed effects

Tags were assigned by the gather passes. One entry can carry several tags and can appear in several craft notes.

## Where sources visibly disagree

- Prerequisite for a roll. The Alexandrian essay on rulings ties a check to a stated player intent plus a clever approach; the Angry GM adjudication article also asks for intent and approach and adds a feasibility gate and a no-cost-retry exemption. The gatherer recorded this as a difference of emphasis, not a contradiction. (https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/4238/roleplaying-games/the-art-of-rulings; https://theangrygm.com/adjudicate-actions-like-a-boss/)

## Edition divergences observed

- SRD 5.2.1 condition records use the term D20 Test (Exhaustion record) and capitalized Advantage and Disadvantage; the DMG 2014 headings inventoried treat ability checks, attack rolls and saving throws as separate sections (Using Ability Scores › Ability Checks, Attack Rolls, Saving Throws) and do not use the term D20 Test.
- DMG 2014 carries Inspiration as a sub-section under Using Ability Scores; the SRD 5.2.1 records read here contain no inspiration text, so the two are not comparable from the material read.
- The SRD 5.2.1 records read here are conditions only; the SRD Playing the Game rules text (d20 tests, DC table, Influence, Search, Study) is not in packages/srd, so DMG 2014 rules sections cannot be compared against it from this pass.
