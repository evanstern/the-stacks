# Rewards and House Rules

_How the sources treat rewards (treasure, experience, boons, downtime) and optional or variant rules. Neutral and descriptive: this note lists what the gathered sources cover. It states no verdicts._

## Tags in this area

- `rewards` — treasure, experience, boons, downtime rewards
- `house-rules` — optional, variant and house rules

Tags were assigned by the gather passes. One entry can carry several tags and can appear in several craft notes.

### Dungeon Master's Guide (DMG 2014, 5th Edition 2014)

- DMG 2014 › Ch 1 A World of Your Own › The Big Picture › It's Your World — Heading-level only. Encourages starting from the core assumptions and changing the ones that do not fit the campaign. _[world-building, house-rules]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 1 A World of Your Own › Factions and Organizations — Heading-level only. Covers building factions, tying characters to them, and tracking renown, ranks, benefits, losses, and piety as a campaign mechanic. _[world-building, rewards]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 4 Creating Nonplayer Characters › NPC Party Members › Optional Rule: Loyalty — Optional secret loyalty score that governs how far an NPC companion will go for the party. _[npcs, secrets-information, house-rules]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 6 Between Adventures › Recurring Expenses — Covers upkeep costs, businesses, and garrisons as gold sinks. _[rewards, world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 6 Between Adventures › Downtime Activities › More Downtime Activities — Covers building a stronghold, carousing, crafting magic items, gaining renown, sacred rites, running a business, selling items, sowing rumors, and training. _[time-tracking, rewards, social-interaction]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 6 Between Adventures › Downtime Activities › Creating Downtime Activities — Guidance for inventing new downtime activities consistent with the existing ones. _[house-rules, improvisation]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 7 Treasure › Types of Treasure — Overview of coins, gems, art objects, and magic items as rewards, with value tables. _[rewards]_

- DMG 2014 › Ch 7 Treasure › Magic Items — Rules-lore on rarity, buying and selling, identification, attunement, curses, categories, activation, charges, resilience, and quirks; individual item lists are out of scope. _[rewards, rulings-vs-rules]_

- DMG 2014 › Ch 7 Treasure › Other Rewards — Introduces non-item rewards from gods, rulers, and other patrons. _[rewards]_

- DMG 2014 › Ch 7 Treasure › Other Rewards › Marks of Prestige — Letters, medals, land, favors, rights, strongholds, titles, and training as social rewards. _[rewards, world-building]_

- DMG 2014 › Ch 8 Running the Game › Using Ability Scores › Inspiration — Treats inspiration as a lever for encouraging roleplay and risk-taking. _[rewards, player-agency]_

### Xanathar's Guide to Everything (XGtE, 2017)

- XGtE › Traps Revisited › Complex Traps › Experience for Complex Traps — XP awards for overcoming complex traps and judging when they count as overcome. _[rewards, hazards-traps]_
- XGtE › Downtime Revisited › Example Downtime Activities — Heading-level only. Buying and selling magic items, carousing, crafting, crime, gambling, pit fighting, relaxation, religious service, research, scribing, training, and work. _[time-tracking, rewards]_
- XGtE › Awarding Magic Items › Awarding Magic Items — Offers rarity-based and level-by-level plans for deciding how many and what kind of magic items to hand out. _[rewards, adventure-design]_
