# Secrets and Information

_How the sources treat hidden information, clues, mysteries, and what the DM reveals to the players. Neutral and descriptive: this note lists what the gathered sources cover. It states no verdicts._

## Tags in this area

- `secrets-information` — hidden information, clues, mysteries

Tags were assigned by the gather passes. One entry can carry several tags and can appear in several craft notes.

## Entries by source

### Dungeon Master's Guide (DMG 2014, 5th Edition 2014)

- DMG 2014 › Ch 3 Creating Adventures › Adventure Types › Mysteries — Explains building a crime-based adventure from victim, suspects, and clues, and being ready for players to solve it in unplanned ways. _[adventure-design, secrets-information]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 4 Creating Nonplayer Characters › Designing NPCs › Detailed NPCs › Useful Knowledge — One piece of information the NPC holds that can help or mislead the party. _[npcs, secrets-information]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 4 Creating Nonplayer Characters › Designing NPCs › Detailed NPCs › Flaw or Secret — A weakness or hidden fact that can undermine the NPC and generate plot. _[npcs, secrets-information]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 4 Creating Nonplayer Characters › NPC Party Members › Optional Rule: Loyalty — Optional secret loyalty score that governs how far an NPC companion will go for the party. _[npcs, secrets-information, house-rules]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 4 Creating Nonplayer Characters › Villains › Villain's Secret Weakness — Teaches giving a villain a hidden weakness that players can discover and exploit. _[npcs, secrets-information, adventure-design]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 6 Between Adventures › Foreshadowing — Teaches subtle hints toward future events, accepting that some will go unused. _[adventure-design, secrets-information]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 8 Running the Game › Exploration › Noticing Other Creatures — Adjudicating who spots whom first. _[exploration, adjudication, secrets-information]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 8 Running the Game › Combat › Tracking Monster Hit Points — Track secretly and consider how to convey a monster's condition through description. _[combat-running, combat-narration, secrets-information]_

### Xanathar's Guide to Everything (XGtE, 2017)

- XGtE › Dungeon Master's Tools › Spellcasting › Perceiving a Caster at Work — Decide what bystanders can observe when a spell is cast and how visible a casting is. _[secrets-information, adjudication]_

### Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (TCoE, 2020)

- TCoE › Ch 4 Dungeon Master's Tools › Parleying with Monsters › Monster Research — Characters can learn what a creature wants via skill checks by monster type, with a DC rule. _[social-interaction, ability-checks, secrets-information]_
- TCoE › Ch 5 Puzzles › Why Use Puzzles? › Running Puzzles — Teaches presenting a puzzle, answering clarifying questions, and not giving away the solution. _[puzzles, secrets-information, improvisation]_

### Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft (VRGtR, 2021)

### Curse of Strahd (CoS, 2016)
