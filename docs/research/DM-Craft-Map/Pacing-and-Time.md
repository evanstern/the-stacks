# Pacing and Time

_How the sources treat the tempo of scenes and sessions and the tracking of in-game time (travel, rests, calendars, clocks). Neutral and descriptive: this note lists what the gathered sources cover. It states no verdicts._

## Tags in this area

- `pacing` — tempo of scenes, sessions and adventures
- `time-tracking` — travel time, rests, calendars and clocks

Tags were assigned by the gather passes. One entry can carry several tags and can appear in several craft notes.

## Entries by source

### Dungeon Master's Guide (DMG 2014, 5th Edition 2014)

- DMG 2014 › Ch 3 Creating Adventures › Adventure Structure — Frames an adventure as beginning (a hook), middle (choices with consequences), and ending (a climax the players shape). _[adventure-design, pacing]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 3 Creating Adventures › Adventure Structure › Ending — Teaches building tension toward a climax that depends on prior successes and failures and resolves the threat. _[adventure-design, pacing]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 3 Creating Adventures › Creating Encounters › Creating a Combat Encounter › The Adventuring Day — Gives a per-character daily XP budget and expected number of fights, with guidance on short rests. _[encounter-design, pacing, time-tracking]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 3 Creating Adventures › Random Encounters › Triggering Random Encounters — Advises choosing placement thoughtfully so random events build on the session instead of competing with it. _[encounter-design, pacing]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 3 Creating Adventures › Random Encounters › Checking for Random Encounters — Describes when to check (per hour, per several hours, per day and rest) or simply decide. _[encounter-design, time-tracking]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 5 Adventure Environments › Wilderness — Introduces wilderness adventures and two ways of pacing travel. _[exploration, pacing]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 5 Adventure Environments › Wilderness › Travel-Montage Approach — Summarize a trip as a montage when the destination matters more than the journey. _[exploration, pacing]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 5 Adventure Environments › Wilderness › Hour-by-Hour Approach — Run travel step by step when the journey itself is the content. _[exploration, pacing, time-tracking]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 5 Adventure Environments › Wilderness Survival — Rules and guidance for weather, altitude, hazards, foraging, food and water, and getting lost. _[exploration, hazards-traps, time-tracking, dc-setting]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 6 Between Adventures › Linking Adventures — Contrasts episodic play with continuity and shows ways to connect adventures. _[adventure-design, pacing]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 6 Between Adventures › Planting Adventure Seeds — Teaches seeding the next adventure before the current one ends. _[adventure-design, pacing]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 6 Between Adventures › Campaign Tracking — Stresses recording details for continuity so the world feels consistent. _[time-tracking, world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 6 Between Adventures › Downtime Activities — Explains allowing days or months to pass between adventures and what characters can do with that time. _[pacing, time-tracking]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 6 Between Adventures › Downtime Activities › More Downtime Activities — Covers building a stronghold, carousing, crafting magic items, gaining renown, sacred rites, running a business, selling items, sowing rumors, and training. _[time-tracking, rewards, social-interaction]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 8 Running the Game › Table Rules › Rolling Attacks and Damage — Suggests rolling attack and damage together to speed play. _[combat-running, pacing]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 8 Running the Game › Table Rules › Rules Discussions — Set a policy on pausing play to debate rules and resolve disputes after the session. _[table-talk, rulings-vs-rules, pacing]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 8 Running the Game › Exploration › Using a Map — Teaches using a map to narrate progress and track pace. _[exploration, time-tracking]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 8 Running the Game › Exploration › Special Travel Pace — Handling parties whose members move at different speeds. _[exploration, time-tracking]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 8 Running the Game › Combat › Tracking Initiative — Hidden list, visible list, and index card methods. _[combat-running, pacing]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 8 Running the Game › Combat › Handling Mobs — Replace many attack rolls with a group-attack shortcut for large crowds. _[combat-running, pacing]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 8 Running the Game › Chases — Turns chases into a structured scene with initiative, limited dashing, complications, and an escape check. _[encounter-design, pacing, combat-running]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 8 Running the Game › Chases › Running the Chase — Dash limits, spells, and attacks during a chase. _[combat-running, pacing]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 8 Running the Game › Experience Points › Level Advancement without XP — Session-based and story-based advancement as alternatives. _[rewards, pacing, house-rules]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 9 Dungeon Master's Workshop › Adventuring Options › Healing — Variants for healer's kits, healing surges, and slower natural healing. _[house-rules, pacing]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 9 Dungeon Master's Workshop › Adventuring Options › Rest Variants — Epic heroism and gritty realism rest lengths to change campaign tone. _[house-rules, tone, time-tracking]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 9 Dungeon Master's Workshop › Combat Options › Initiative Variants — Initiative scores, side initiative, and speed factor as alternatives. _[combat-running, pacing]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 9 Dungeon Master's Workshop › Combat Options › Cleaving through Creatures — Option to carry overkill damage to nearby foes. _[combat-running, pacing]_
- DMG 2014 › Appendix A Random Dungeons › Random Dungeons › Empty Rooms — Note that empty rooms give players a place to rest and pace the dungeon. _[pacing, exploration]_

### Xanathar's Guide to Everything (XGtE, 2017)

- XGtE › Dungeon Master's Tools › Falling › Rate of Falling — Treats falling from great heights as taking time rather than being instantaneous. _[hazards-traps, time-tracking]_
- XGtE › Dungeon Master's Tools › Sleep — Covers how sleeping creatures wake, effects of sleeping in armor, and consequences of skipping long rests. _[time-tracking, conditions-effects, adjudication]_
- XGtE › Downtime Revisited › Downtime Activities — Frames activities lasting a workweek or more that need checks and may trigger complications. _[time-tracking, adventure-design]_
- XGtE › Downtime Revisited › Example Downtime Activities — Heading-level only. Buying and selling magic items, carousing, crafting, crime, gambling, pit fighting, relaxation, religious service, research, scribing, training, and work. _[time-tracking, rewards]_

### Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft (VRGtR, 2021)

- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Running Horror Games — Frames tension as a product of atmosphere, pacing, description, and player involvement. _[horror, pacing, tone]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Running Horror Games › Horror Pacing — Teaches alternating tension and relief so players do not go numb. _[horror, pacing]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Running Horror Games › Horror Pacing › Logistics — Plan for less table time than the full session. _[pacing, time-tracking]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Running Horror Games › Horror Pacing › Idle Uneasiness — Slow down to linger on sensory detail and ask players what they fear. _[horror, pacing, description-narration]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Running Horror Games › Horror Pacing › Out of Time — Speed up narration and demand quick decisions in crises. _[horror, pacing, description-narration]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Running Horror Games › Horror Pacing › Parallel Scenes — Cut between split party scenes and leave players waiting at peaks. _[pacing, multiple-players, spotlight]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Running Horror Games › Horror Pacing › End on Cliffhangers — End sessions on a new threat or twist. _[pacing, adventure-design]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Running Horror Games › Horror Gameplay › Encourage Space — Plan breaks and check whether everyone wants to continue. _[safety-tools, pacing]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Fear and Stress › Stress — Stress accumulates and impairs performance; includes ways to reduce it. _[horror, conditions-effects, time-tracking]_
