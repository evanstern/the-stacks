# DM-Craft-Map

_A breadth map of Dungeon Master craft, gathered from six 5th-edition books, four web sources, the SRD 5.2.1 condition records, and the-stacks project doctrine. It exists to scope later deep-dive branches. Descriptive only: no verdicts._

## Scope

- In: what DMs do at the table and in preparation (adjudication, narration, players, pacing, NPCs, exploration, combat, encounter and adventure design, world and tone, rewards, secrets), as covered by the gathered sources.
- In: the-stacks AI DM components, stated doctrine and known gaps, as a neutral inventory.
- Out: setting lore, monster, spell and magic-item content; verdicts and recommendations (a later analysis).
- First planned deep dive: how a DM decides what the dice decide versus what the player decides, and how each is narrated.

## What is known

- 606 gathered entries: DMG 2014 234; XGtE 44; TCoE 38; VRGtR 84; CoS 13; LMoP 10; SRD 5.2.1 15; Alexandrian 47; Angry GM 33; Sly Flourish 43; Colville (readable URL) 11; Colville (no readable source) 34.
- Entries are grouped into 14 craft areas by tag; the [[Coverage-Matrix|Coverage Matrix]] gives counts per area and source and whether the project doctrine inventory lists a stated rule.
- The SRD 5.2.1 package in the repo holds condition records but not the Playing the Game rules text.
- Web depth is mostly title-level; Colville videos were not read.
