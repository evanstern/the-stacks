# Players, Agency and the Table

_How the sources treat the players as participants: their choices, their expectations and play styles, groups of several players, shared attention, and conversation at the table. Neutral and descriptive: this note lists what the gathered sources cover. It states no verdicts._

## Tags in this area

- `player-agency` — player choice and its limits
- `players-and-expectations` — player types, motivations, expectations, table behavior
- `multiple-players` — several players or groups at one table
- `spotlight` — distribution of attention among players
- `table-talk` — conversation, interruptions and rules talk at the table

Tags were assigned by the gather passes. One entry can carry several tags and can appear in several craft notes.

### Dungeon Master's Guide (DMG 2014, 5th Edition 2014)

- DMG 2014 › Ch 3 Creating Adventures › Elements of a Great Adventure — Names the ingredients of a strong adventure: a credible threat, familiar tropes with twists, focus on the present, meaningful hero choices, content for each player type, surprises, and useful maps. _[adventure-design, player-agency, players-and-expectations]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 3 Creating Adventures › Adventure Structure › Middle — Teaches letting each challenge present real choices that visibly affect where the story ends up. _[adventure-design, player-agency]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 3 Creating Adventures › Adventure Types › Event-Based Adventures — Recipe built around a villain's timeline: choose the villain, script their actions, set party goals, anticipate the villain's reactions to interference, and detail only the places that matter. _[adventure-design, npcs, player-agency]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 3 Creating Adventures › Complications › Moral Quandaries — Teaches adding dilemmas of conscience (ally, friend, honor, rescue, respect) that cannot be solved by force or spells. _[adventure-design, player-agency]_

- DMG 2014 › Ch 4 Creating Nonplayer Characters › NPC Party Members › Adventurer NPCs — Covers filling out a short-handed party with NPCs built to the party's level. _[npcs, players-and-expectations]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 5 Adventure Environments › Traps › Traps in Play › Detecting and Disabling a Trap — Uses perception, investigation, and tool checks, and rewards clever player descriptions. _[hazards-traps, ability-checks, player-agency]_
