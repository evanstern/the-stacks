# World and Tone

_How the sources treat building the setting (worlds, planes, factions, settlements) and setting the mood of play. Neutral and descriptive: this note lists what the gathered sources cover. It states no verdicts._

## Tags in this area

- `world-building` — setting, cosmology, factions
- `tone` — mood and atmosphere

Tags were assigned by the gather passes. One entry can carry several tags and can appear in several craft notes.

## Entries by source

### Dungeon Master's Guide (DMG 2014, 5th Edition 2014)

- DMG 2014 › Ch 1 A World of Your Own › The Big Picture — Heading-level only. Frames the default assumptions of D&D worlds and invites the DM to alter them deliberately when building a setting. _[world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 1 A World of Your Own › The Big Picture › Core Assumptions — Heading-level only. Lists the baseline facts the rules presume about a game world, such as real gods and a dangerous wild frontier. _[world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 1 A World of Your Own › The Big Picture › It's Your World — Heading-level only. Encourages starting from the core assumptions and changing the ones that do not fit the campaign. _[world-building, house-rules]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 1 A World of Your Own › Gods of Your World — Heading-level only. Covers how to structure deities and religion in a campaign, from loose pantheons to monotheism and philosophies. _[world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 1 A World of Your Own › Mapping Your Campaign — Heading-level only. Covers drawing a campaign map at province, kingdom, or continent scale and combining the scales. _[world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 1 A World of Your Own › Settlements — Heading-level only. Covers a settlement's purpose in play (color, home base, adventure site), size, atmosphere, government, commerce, and currency. _[world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 1 A World of Your Own › Languages and Dialects — Heading-level only. Short note on deciding which tongues exist in the world and how they relate. _[world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 1 A World of Your Own › Factions and Organizations — Heading-level only. Covers building factions, tying characters to them, and tracking renown, ranks, benefits, losses, and piety as a campaign mechanic. _[world-building, rewards]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 1 A World of Your Own › Magic in Your World — Heading-level only. Covers deciding how common magic is, restricting it, teleportation circles, and the rules for resurrection in the setting. _[world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 2 Creating a Multiverse › The Planes — Heading-level only. Surveys planar categories and ways to arrange the planes into a cosmology. _[world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 2 Creating a Multiverse › Planar Travel — Heading-level only. Covers portals and spells as means to move between planes. _[world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 2 Creating a Multiverse › Astral Plane — Heading-level only. Plane description with travel rules, color pools, psychic wind, and encounters. _[world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 2 Creating a Multiverse › Ethereal Plane — Heading-level only. Plane description with border and deep regions, ether cyclones, and encounters. _[world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 2 Creating a Multiverse › Feywild — Heading-level only. Plane description with crossings and optional rules for memory loss and time distortion that a DM can impose. _[world-building, conditions-effects]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 2 Creating a Multiverse › Shadowfell — Heading-level only. Plane description including domains of dread and an optional despair rule. _[world-building, horror]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 2 Creating a Multiverse › Inner Planes — Heading-level only. Short descriptions of the elemental planes. _[world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 2 Creating a Multiverse › Outer Planes — Heading-level only. Describes each outer plane, with a recurring pattern of optional rules that make a plane's nature felt mechanically. _[world-building, conditions-effects]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 2 Creating a Multiverse › Other Planes — Heading-level only. Covers the Outlands, Sigil, demiplanes, and the Far Realm. _[world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 2 Creating a Multiverse › Known Worlds of the Material Plane — Heading-level only. Brief pointer to published campaign worlds. _[world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 5 Adventure Environments › Mapping a Wilderness — Covers map movement, level of narrative detail, and placing lairs, monuments, ruins, settlements, strongholds, and weird locales. _[exploration, world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 5 Adventure Environments › Settlements (Towns, Villages, Cities) — Frames settlements as adventure backdrops and provides random tables for traits, buildings, and calamities. _[world-building, adventure-design, improvisation]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 6 Between Adventures › Campaign Tracking — Stresses recording details for continuity so the world feels consistent. _[time-tracking, world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 6 Between Adventures › Recurring Expenses — Covers upkeep costs, businesses, and garrisons as gold sinks. _[rewards, world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 7 Treasure › Other Rewards › Marks of Prestige — Letters, medals, land, favors, rights, strongholds, titles, and training as social rewards. _[rewards, world-building]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 9 Dungeon Master's Workshop › Ability Options › Hero Points — Option for a mythic, superheroic tone. _[house-rules, tone]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 9 Dungeon Master's Workshop › Ability Options › New Ability Scores: Honor and Sanity — Add Honor or Sanity scores for code-bound or cosmic horror campaigns. _[house-rules, horror, tone]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 9 Dungeon Master's Workshop › Adventuring Options › Fear and Horror — Optional saves for facing fear and horror to sustain dread. _[horror, conditions-effects, tone]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 9 Dungeon Master's Workshop › Adventuring Options › Rest Variants — Epic heroism and gritty realism rest lengths to change campaign tone. _[house-rules, tone, time-tracking]_
- DMG 2014 › Ch 9 Dungeon Master's Workshop › Combat Options › Injuries — Optional lingering injuries, massive damage, and system shock. _[conditions-effects, house-rules, tone]_

### Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (TCoE, 2020)

- TCoE › Using This Book › Using This Book › Ten Rules to Remember › 10. Have Fun — Encourages following the group's preferred style over strict rules mastery. _[players-and-expectations, tone]_
- TCoE › Ch 4 Dungeon Master's Tools › Session Zero › Game Customization — Customize the game to player tastes using preferences learned up front. _[session-zero, players-and-expectations, tone]_
- TCoE › Ch 4 Dungeon Master's Tools › Environmental Hazards — Presents unusual regions, magical phenomena, and natural hazards for adventures. _[hazards-traps, world-building]_
- TCoE › Ch 4 Dungeon Master's Tools › Environmental Hazards › Supernatural Regions — Seven region types, such as haunted or infested, that change what a place does to those within it. _[hazards-traps, world-building, conditions-effects]_

### Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft (VRGtR, 2021)

- VRGtR › Welcome to Ravenloft › Using This Book › Horror in Your Game — Prompts the DM to decide what role horror plays in the campaign and previews the book's tools for fear, genre, and setting. _[horror, tone, session-zero]_
- VRGtR › Welcome to Ravenloft › Using This Book › Seven Secrets of Ravenloft — Lists the setting's foundational truths the DM should understand before running it. _[horror, world-building]_
- VRGtR › Welcome to Ravenloft › The Land of the Mists › Nightmare Logic — Explains the setting's deliberately inconsistent reality and how it supports dread. _[horror, tone]_
- VRGtR › Welcome to Ravenloft › The Land of the Mists › The Dark Powers — Describes the hidden architects of the setting; the DM chooses what they are. _[horror, secrets-information, world-building]_
- VRGtR › Welcome to Ravenloft › The Land of the Mists › The Mists — Describes mist borders and how the DM can use them. _[horror, world-building]_
- VRGtR › Welcome to Ravenloft › The Land of the Mists › Domains — Describes the shared features of Domains of Dread. _[horror, world-building]_
- VRGtR › Creating Domains of Dread › Creating a Darklord › Corrupt Beyond Redemption — Make the villain culpable through choices, not circumstance. _[npcs, horror, tone]_
- VRGtR › Creating Domains of Dread › Creating a Domain — Teaches making setting details reflect the villain's evil and torment. _[horror, world-building, adventure-design]_
- VRGtR › Creating Domains of Dread › Creating a Domain › Darklord's Shadows — Everything in the domain echoes the villain. _[horror, world-building]_
- VRGtR › Creating Domains of Dread › Creating a Domain › Domain Overview — Steps for selecting locations tied to the villain's past. _[horror, world-building]_
- VRGtR › Creating Domains of Dread › Creating a Domain › Cultural Specifics — Build the culture around the villain's torment. _[world-building, horror]_
- VRGtR › Creating Domains of Dread › Creating a Domain › Misty Borders — Decide how the Mists behave at the edges. _[horror, world-building]_
- VRGtR › Genres of Horror › Genres of Horror — Introduces horror subgenres as inspiration for villains, domains, and plots, each with a repeated set of subsections. _[horror, tone, adventure-design]_
- VRGtR › Genres of Horror › Body Horror — Fear of failing anatomy; supplies monster, villain, torment, setting, site, and plot ideas. _[horror, tone]_
- VRGtR › Genres of Horror › Cosmic Horror — Fear of insignificance before incomprehensible entities, with the same structure of ideas. _[horror, tone]_
- VRGtR › Genres of Horror › Dark Fantasy — Fantasy steeped in horror elements; same structure of ideas. _[horror, tone]_
- VRGtR › Genres of Horror › Folk Horror — Fear arising from inherited traditions and communities; same structure of ideas. _[horror, tone]_
- VRGtR › Genres of Horror › Ghost Stories — Horror of unresolved past wrongs; same structure of ideas. _[horror, tone]_
- VRGtR › Genres of Horror › Gothic Horror — Horror of inner depravity beneath civility; same structure of ideas. _[horror, tone]_
- VRGtR › Genres of Horror › Other Horror Genres — Short notes on disaster, occult detective, psychological, and slasher horror. _[horror, tone]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Preparing for Horror › Understanding Horror — The DM's job is a fun experience; players enjoy fear because they trust the DM to keep it safe. _[horror, safety-tools, tone]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Preparing for Horror › Session Zero › Customize Your Experience — Discuss genre, campaign themes, and how to adapt rules for the group. _[session-zero, tone, house-rules]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Running Horror Games — Frames tension as a product of atmosphere, pacing, description, and player involvement. _[horror, pacing, tone]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Running Horror Games › Horror Atmosphere — Teaches shaping mood through the physical space and the DM's own behavior. _[horror, tone, description-narration]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Running Horror Games › Horror Atmosphere › Location — Pick a space with fewer distractions. _[tone]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Running Horror Games › Horror Atmosphere › Music — Use quiet instrumental audio to fill lulls. _[tone]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Running Horror Games › Horror Atmosphere › Lighting — Dim lighting thoughtfully, keeping text readable. _[tone]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Running Horror Games › Horror Atmosphere › Props — Keep props simple and optional. _[tone]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Running Horror Games › Horror Atmosphere › The Dungeon Master — The DM models behavior, voice, and pacing that set the tone. _[tone, description-narration]_
- VRGtR › Horror Adventures › Running Horror Games › Horror Pacing › Reliability and Trust — Keep some allies and places reliable to avoid paralyzing paranoia. _[npcs, tone, player-agency]_
