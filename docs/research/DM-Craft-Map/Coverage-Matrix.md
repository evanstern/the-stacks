## Method

- Counts come from the gathered entries after source-handling rules were applied (see [[_grounding]]).
- Colville entries are split: entries with a readable URL (podcast index pages) and titles without a readable source. Both are counted, in separate columns.
- SRD 5.2.1 counts the 15 condition records only.
- The doctrine column answers only whether A3 lists a stated rule for the tag; it does not measure how well a rule is enforced.
