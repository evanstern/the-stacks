# Brief and Assumptions

## Branch question

How does a DM run NPCs and the information they carry: attitude as persistent state, social checks and their DCs, NPC knowledge limits and lying, the clue economy, and who on the DM side may read an NPC's secrets?

## Origin

Card TASK-0119 (research branch 2). The ordering basis is the TASK-0115 analysis `Analysis-Refactor-Risk-and-Deep-Dive-Order` in the DM-Craft-Map branch (operator-approved 2026-10-26): branch 2 is contract-class because attitude would be persistent state, and the clue economy and the DM-only view both pull on the seed schema. The card also folds in TASK-0107 (DM-side nodes read NPC secrets), gathered here as facts only. This branch is the EMBED phase: it states no verdict and writes no analysis.

## Sub-questions

1. Attitude: is NPC attitude persistent state, and how do sources change it? See [[Attitudes-as-Persistent-State]].
2. Checks: when to roll, which check, how the DC is set, what retry and failure do. See [[Social-Checks-and-DCs]].
3. Knowledge and lying: what an NPC knows, may withhold or lie about, and how players detect it. See [[NPC-Knowledge-Limits-and-Lying]].
4. Clues: how information is spread and made redundant. See [[Clue-Economy]].
5. Records: what data sources give an NPC and which is hidden. See [[NPC-Records-and-Motives]].
6. TASK-0107: who reads NPC secrets in the-stacks today and what the sources and code say about a DM-only view. See [[DM-Side-Reading-of-NPC-Secrets]].
