# NPCs, Social Interaction and Information

_How a DM runs non-player characters as persistent actors, resolves social checks, decides what an NPC knows and says (including lies), and spreads clues, drawn from SRD 5.2.1, DMG 2014, three further books, web sources and the-stacks project state. Descriptive: this branch states no verdicts._

## Scope

In: NPC attitude as persistent state; social checks and their DCs; NPC knowledge limits and lying; the clue economy (Three Clue Rule and kin); how the-stacks stores NPC secrets and who reads them (the TASK-0107 question, gathered as facts); NPC records and motives as sources describe them; how the-stacks stores and uses NPC data today.

Out: resolution and outcome in general (see the Resolution and Outcome branch), exploration and time, the table contract, running combat, encounter and adventure design in full, world and tone, rewards.

## What is known

- SRD 5.2.1 gives NPCs one of three attitudes (Friendly, Indifferent, Hostile), says a monster's default is Indifferent, and ties a die roll to the Influence action, with Advantage when Friendly and Disadvantage when Hostile. DMG 2014 gives a four-step structure and a DC-by-attitude table instead. See [[Attitudes-as-Persistent-State]] and [[Social-Checks-and-DCs]].
- No source in the gathered set gives a fixed rule for NPC lies or for what an NPC cannot know. The books treat knowledge as a designed NPC field; web sources treat lying as a motive-and-signal problem. See [[NPC-Knowledge-Limits-and-Lying]].
- The clue economy is described as redundancy (Three Clue Rule), as automatic delivery of essential information (GUMSHOE core clues), and as short secrets improvised into play (Lazy DM). The project's own doctrine pair says at most one clue per beat. See [[Clue-Economy]].
- Sources split NPC data into public-facing traits and hidden data (secrets, flaws, loyalty). See [[NPC-Records-and-Motives]].
- The-stacks holds NPC secrets in a DM-only seed field that no prompt reads, no state holds an attitude, and seed beat clues have no reader. See [[The-Stacks-NPCs-and-Information-Today]] and [[DM-Side-Reading-of-NPC-Secrets]].
