# Resolution and Outcome

_How a DM decides what the dice decide versus what the player decides, and how each is narrated, drawn from SRD 5.2.1, four books, three web sources and the-stacks project doctrine. Descriptive: this branch states no verdicts._

## Scope

In: when to roll; how DCs and modifiers are set; outcome shapes beyond pass and fail; failure, consequences and retries; contests, group checks and Help; what a roll or effect may impose on a player character; narrating outcomes; out-of-character questions to the DM; pacing and spotlight as they touch resolution; how the-stacks resolves and narrates actions today.

Out: NPC social design, exploration and time, the table contract in full, running combat, encounter and adventure design, world and tone, rewards and house rules. These are separate branches.

## What is known

- SRD 5.2.1 states that the GM sets DCs, decides whether a D20 Test is warranted, and tells players the result. Dice enter where an outcome is uncertain and failure is meaningful. See [[When-to-Roll]] and [[Setting-DCs-and-Modifiers]].
- Outcomes extend past pass and fail in DMG 2014 (success at a cost, degrees of failure, scaled results on a 1 or 20), and in the web sources (failing forward, succeed-but-roll-anyway, progress clocks). See [[Outcome-Shapes]] and [[Failure-Consequences-and-Retries]].
- Opposed efforts, group checks and Help are treated differently across DMG 2014, SRD 5.2.1 and the web sources. See [[Contests-Group-Checks-and-Help]].
- SRD 5.2.1 conditions and effects remove capability, movement or action; only a few spells, Demonic Possession and DMG 2014 madness tables dictate behavior. See [[Involuntary-Effects-and-Player-Choice]].
- The sources split narration by who declares, who determines and who describes. See [[Narrating-Outcomes]], [[Table-Talk-and-Questions-to-the-DM]] and [[Pacing-and-Spotlight-in-Resolution]].
- The-stacks states a resolution pipeline with an engine that owns numbers and a model that supplies modifiers and DCs as data. See [[The-Stacks-Resolution-Today]].
