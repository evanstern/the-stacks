# Onboarding and Design Writing

_How MUDs onboard new players, and published design commentary found._

## In-game onboarding (Discworld MUD)

- New characters start in a separate area with limited inventory and money. `help`, `help essentials`, `help here` (room-specific), `syntax <command>`; a `newbie` channel for players under 18 hours; `helpers`/`liaisons` list online helpers; `godmother help` returns to the start; players are pointed to a talker after the channel closes ([Getting started](https://discworld.starturtle.net/lpc/playing/getting_started.html); [essentials](http://discworld.starturtle.net/lpc/playing/documentation.c?path=%2Fnewbie%2Fessentials); [newbie channel](https://discworld.starturtle.net/lpc/playing/documentation.c?path=%2Fconcepts%2Fcreator%2Fnewbie_channel)).
- Dune MUD offers a newbie page with brief settings applied via login commands ([Dune newbies](https://dunemud.net/newbies)).

## Writing by designers

- Raph Koster, "In the newbie's shoes" (2006): newbies are the audience least like designers; a good interface works for veterans and newbies alike; he describes placing player cities near newbie zones so newbies meet experienced players early ([post](https://www.raphkoster.com/2006/09/19/in-the-newbies-shoes/)). In an interview he recounts leading a newbie-helping guild on his first MUD, where admins notified the leader of new arrivals ([LiveOps interview](https://www.raphkoster.com/games/interviews-and-panels/the-art-of-liveops-raph-koster/)).
- Bartle's *Designing Virtual Worlds* (2003) is hosted on the Internet Archive; the gathered search did not surface its interface chapters ([Archive](https://archive.org/details/richard-bartle-designing-virtual-worlds)). His history paper describes MUD1 (1978) as text where everything experienced was reported in words, a multiplayer development of Colossal Cave and Zork ([Bartle paper](https://mud.co.uk/richard/International%20Handbook%20of%20Internet%20Research.pdf)).
- Progressive reveal (A Dark Room) and quality gating (Fallen London) are documented in [[Adjacent-Text-Games]].
