Plan: foundations first, lanes in parallel behind frozen contracts

Sequence by shared foundations, not by goal. Freeze each seam contract before any lane builds against it — a frozen contract is what makes two lanes safe in parallel. Every milestone ends in a demo and, where play is involved, a hosted playtest.
