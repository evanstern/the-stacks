VTT: hybrid, build-led

Build a small in-app tactical grid that is a pure projection of the event log. Keep Universal VTT export. Keep exactly one live adapter (Foundry first) behind a trigger. Do not embed an open-source VTT.

research/vtt-research.md §0, §4, §6, §7, §8 · decisions-for-ratification.md P-02, P-05, P-06, P-07

Why build

No external VTT keeps a secret

Foundry and Owlbear hide tokens in the client. An AI DM with hidden monsters needs server-side withholding.

No embed has a usable licence and model

Owlbear legacy is non-commercial; MapTool and Atlas are AGPL; PlanarAlly brings its own server and state.

The grid we need is small

Maps are structured data first (MapPlan), so walls, rooms and fog are known by construction.

AI-operability decides it

The AI DM reads and writes the map through the same gate as dice and HP.

Three views of one state

Theatre-of-the-mind, zones and grid are projection settings, not modes. One combat moves between them without migrating state. A token without a square sits in its zone.

Phases V0–V4

Adapter trigger: a named table or issue asks for it, or an R5 playtest shows a group refusing to leave its VTT. Review at CP-15.

Skipped: hex grids, elevation, multi-floor, drawing tools, animated effects. Each gets a revisit card with a trigger.
