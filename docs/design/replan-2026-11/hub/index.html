Morning briefing · 2026-11-07

The board has 135 problems. Underneath them are five missing foundations.

You asked for the product plan reworked from the four end goals backward. The overnight run produced the research, the synthesis, a proposed constitution 3.0.0 and a board rework. Nothing binds until you rule at the checkpoints below.

Thesis. Six weeks of AI-first work made one scene of good narration and no code for the codex, sheets, VTT, generators or human-DM tools. Most of the 135 open cards are symptoms of five missing foundations: a spatial model (F1), a typed Intent vocabulary with a deterministic planner (F2), a campaign/codex data model (F3) that also adds campaign, table and session records, memory and continuity (F4), and narration rendered around code facts (F5). Build those once and all four goals share them. Latency, not cost, is the binding constraint: median turns ran 28–39 s, and players leave at 30 s. So F2 and F5 come first.
